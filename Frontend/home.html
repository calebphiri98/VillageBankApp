About Manase VSLA

A digital solution for Village Savings and Loan Associations

Building Financial Strength Together

Manase VSLA supports community-based savings groups by replacing paper records with a secure, easy-to-use digital system.

Committee members can manage savings, approve loans, record fines, track welfare support, and perform end-of-cycle share-outs — all in one place.

The system is designed to be simple enough for everyday use on phones and computers, while providing clear financial records for accountability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about the Manase VSLA system

What is a VSLA?
A Village Savings and Loan Association (VSLA) is a community group where members save money together, borrow when needed, and share the profits at the end of a cycle.
Who can use this system?
The system is mainly used by committee members (Chairperson, Treasurer, Secretary) and administrators. Ordinary members can also view their own records when given access.
How is the maximum loan calculated?
A member can borrow up to 3 times their total savings. This protects the group fund and encourages regular saving.
Can the system send SMS notifications?
Yes. The system can send SMS alerts when savings are recorded, loans are approved, repayments are made, fines are issued, or meetings are scheduled.
Is the system mobile-friendly?
Yes. The system works on smartphones, tablets, and computers through a web browser, making it easy for users in the community to access it.
