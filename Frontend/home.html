Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Manase VSLA

What is a VSLA?
A Village Savings and Loan Association is a community group where members save together, borrow when needed, and share profits at cycle end.
How do I join?
Submit a Request to Join on this page. The committee reviews and approves requests, then creates your membership account.
How is the maximum loan calculated?
Members can borrow up to 3 times their total savings to protect the group fund and encourage regular saving.
Can the public see group funds?
No. Money amounts stay private. Only public activity graphs and rates are shown. Full finances appear after login for authorized users.
Can members view their own records?
Yes. Members can log in to My Profile to view savings, loans, fines, documents, and request a loan.
